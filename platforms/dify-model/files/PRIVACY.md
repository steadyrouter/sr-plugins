# Privacy

This plugin sends your requests to SteadyRouter (`api.steadyrouter.com`), so that the model you choose can answer them.

**What is sent**: the prompts, messages, files and parameters of each request, and your SteadyRouter API key to identify your account.

**Who receives it**: SteadyRouter, which forwards the request to the upstream provider that serves the model. SteadyRouter's own handling of your data is described in its [Privacy Policy]({{privacy_url}}).

**What the plugin stores**: nothing. It keeps no copy of your requests or replies, collects no analytics, and does not log your key. Dify stores the key in its own credential system.
